## How to make each Short (about 1–2 hours each)
1. Open Google Flow, set it to **9:16**, and paste **Clip 1**'s prompt. Generate it, then do the same for clips 2–6 in order. If your Flow plan lets you add reference images, attach a good frame of Grok from clip 1 to the later clips so the characters stay consistent.
2. Re-roll any clip where a character's look changes, extra limbs or people appear, or text shows up on screen.
3. Listen to all six clips back to back. If one clip's narrator sounds different, regenerate just that clip.
4. Edit in CapCut (or any editor): put the clips in order, trim at the joins, add the text overlays listed in each file, and turn on auto-captions.
5. Upload as a Short using the title, description and pinned comment from the file. Schedule it in YouTube Studio for the day in the table above.

**Series hook:** Grok is the recurring inventor whose ideas keep backfiring, and the saber-tooth tiger keeps coming back to steal the food. Future episodes could cover the first caveman to invent Wi-Fi, homework or the group chat.
