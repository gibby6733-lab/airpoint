# Episode 3 — Your Brain vs. You at 3 AM
**Post:** Friday · **Series:** Relatable / Meme · **Format:** 9:16 Short · 60 s (6 × ~10 s Google Flow clips) · **Style:** A4 Cozy Storytelling (full-color cartoon)

> **Why A4 instead of B6:** B6 is rendered on this machine and needs an image generator plus an ElevenLabs voice, neither of which is set up yet — and it's a much longer build per video. A4 gives the same full-color cartoon comedy feel and is made in Google Flow exactly like Episodes 1–2, so all three can ship this week. We can switch the meme series to B6 once those services are connected.

## Global continuity (summary — every prompt below repeats these in full)
- **Cast:** the Sleeper (protagonist) and the Brain in party hat; the Stranger and Friend (clip 3), the Waiter (clip 4), three fish (clip 5).
- **Settings (one new place per clip, as this style intends):** night bedroom → Brain HQ inside the head → sunny sidewalk flashback → restaurant flashback → underwater dream (back to bed) → **planned return** to the bedroom, next morning.
- **Props:** plain-faced alarm clock (no numerals) at 3 (1) and 7 (6); puffy blue blanket travels with the Sleeper (1, 2, 5, 6); microphone + projector (2); noodle bowl (4); tiny cup (5); juice box (5).
- **Narrator:** the same calm, deadpan male narrator as Episodes 1–2 — one channel voice. **Music:** music-box lullaby that turns bouncy (plucked strings + clarinet) when the Brain takes over.
