    Strategy lab: chance of passing

    Tuned period Unseen period Coin-flip 28%

    From the walk-forward run on 750 days of simulated MNQ: each strategy tuned on the first 60% of days, then run unchanged with its sizing plan on the last 40%. Simulated markets can't prove an edge. Re-run on real 1-minute data with python -m propbot.research --csv your_mnq.csv.

    What the research says

    Lessons so far

    • Backtest winners mostly fell apart on unseen days. VWAP reversion looked best when tuned (44% pass) and collapsed to 8.5% on new data. Always judge a strategy on days it was not tuned on.
    • ±5% around 28% is noise. On a pure random walk with nothing to find, the "best" strategy still scored 33% on unseen days.
    • Prior-day levels is the one to test on real data. It was the only setup that stayed profitable (profit factor 1.07) on unseen simulated days, at 35% pass with 4 micros, a $450 daily stop and a $625 profit cap.
    • Size to your edge. With no edge, small size mostly runs out of time, and a few large trades are a coin flip (~45% pass). Small size only wins when the strategy has a real, proven edge.
    • Bank at most $625 a day. That keeps you inside the 50% consistency rule, so hitting $1,250 actually passes.
