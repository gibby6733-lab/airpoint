# Fogville reel

27.6s vertical (1080x1920) Instagram/TikTok reel for AirPoint, "Fogville" concept.

- `fogville-reel.mp4`: final video
- `scene.html`: canvas animation (`renderFrame(t)`)
- `audio.py`: synthesizes the music and sound effects (`music.wav`)
- `render.js`: renders frames with Playwright and muxes them with the voiceover (`vo1.mp3`, ElevenLabs) and the music via ffmpeg

The DM figures in the phone mockup (11°C, "HIGH") are illustrative examples.
