Galloping Gertie didn't collapse from resonance. The wind was steady, not pulsing. The real cause was aeroelastic flutter: every twist changed how the wind hit the deck, feeding even more twist. The only casualty was a dog named Tubby.

## Upload checklist
- **YouTube Shorts:** upload the `.mp4`. When you set the thumbnail in the YouTube app, upload the `_thumbnail.png` if your app offers a custom-image option. If it only lets you pick a frame, use the first frame, which already shows the big hook card. Put the title in the title field and the description plus hashtags below it.
- **TikTok:** upload the `.mp4`, then **Edit cover → Upload** and pick the `_thumbnail.png`. Use the title as the first line of the caption, followed by the hashtags.
- **Pin a comment** asking the question from the description to drive replies.
- **After 48 hours:** check "Viewed vs. swiped away" in YouTube Analytics. Anything above 70% means that format is working, so make more like it.
