# Your Move Studio: upload kit for 30 Shorts
Post **1 Short a day at about 6 PM** your local time, starting Sun Nov 15, 2026. Schedule them in YouTube Studio (Upload, Visibility, Schedule). Every Short ends with a question, so pin the comment below each one.
**Custom thumbnail:** for Shorts you choose the cover in the **YouTube mobile app** when uploading (the thumbnail button). Use the matching file in `thumbnails/`.
**Captions:** the subtitles are burned into each video. You can also upload the matching `.srt` file under Subtitles.
