# "What If" survival series — style guide (from studying 21 what_if_survival reels)

How the reference creator works (what we copy as *format*, never his stories):
- **Series of 3-7 parts**, posted daily, each ~1:30-2:00. Title card: "WHAT IF YOU ___? PART N".
- **Second person** ("you"). Calm, deep, deliberate narrator (energy 3/5). Short sentences, pauses. No hype.
- **Part 1** opens on a cold hook line with numbers ("Forty-one dead are waiting at the gate…"), then shows the base
  as a cutaway + its RULES ("RULE 1: THE HOUSE MUST LOOK DEAD"), supplies inventory, then a twist.
- **Part 2+** open with the poll result: "84% of you voted hide." on a YOU VOTED bar card, then title card.
- **Real facts as weapons**, each on a paper card, often with a source: gallon/day, candle seen 1.6 mi, sound carries
  farther at night, water heater = 40 gal, honey never spoils, 1865 cans still safe, refeeding: eat small.
- Every part: 1-2 twists, a callback to earlier rules/numbers, recurring named characters, a ticking number
  (days, gallons, tries left, minutes of air).
- **Ending**: two plain options, then cut off mid-sentence (loop). We add our poll question (user's rule).
- Visuals: stick-figure-ish survivor (beanie, green jacket), cutaways, CCTV/phone screens, paper infographic cards,
  red/green/black label badges, counters, maps with distances, day markers. Hard cut every 1-3 s.
- Captions: short phrases, white with yellow keyword. Dark synth drone + an SFX on every action.

Our rules on top: exactly **1:30** per part, **7 parts** per series, ONE would-you-rather at the end (answers said
plainly), user posts the poll, poll winner drives the next part.
