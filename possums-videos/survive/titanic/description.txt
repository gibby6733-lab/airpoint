Would YOU have survived the Titanic? 🚢🧊

April 14, 1912. 11:40 PM. The "unsinkable" ship just hit an iceberg, and there are lifeboat seats for only about half of the 2,224 people on board.

You get 3 life-or-death choices:
1️⃣ Get in the first lifeboat, or stay on the warm ship?
2️⃣ Jump into the ocean, or climb to the back of the ship?
3️⃣ Swim to a flipped lifeboat, or float and wait for rescue?

What really happened:
✅ The first lifeboat left with just 28 people. It had room for 65.
❄️ The water was about -2°C (28°F). Most people who went in early lasted only minutes.
🛟 About 30 men survived the night standing on an overturned lifeboat until the Carpathia arrived around 4 AM.

About 1,500 people died that night. Around 700 survived.
